About the toolkit

Decision support for an FPL Draft season

This site turns the public FPL and FPL Draft data for this league into a season-long decision-support view. It is designed to answer practical questions such as who is available, which players have the best upcoming fixtures, who may be worth stashing, and what has changed around the league.

How it works

A scheduled GitHub Actions job collects the latest Draft league ownership, player status and Premier League fixture data. It stores a small ownership snapshot so changes can be detected between runs, then publishes the processed results to this GitHub Pages site.

Current scoring model

The first-pass v0.1 model is intentionally transparent rather than a black box. Roster score combines position-relative historical FPL points, the next four Gameweeks of official FPL fixture difficulty and current availability. Stash score shifts more weight toward future fixtures and only lightly penalizes a player for being unavailable right now.

What the main views are for

  • My Team: your 15 owned players, upcoming fixtures and player-level intelligence.
  • Available: the free-agent pool, sortable by roster value, stash value, fixtures, points and fitness.
  • League Activity: ownership changes detected between snapshots, intended to become an opponent add/drop radar.
  • Planner: a multi-Gameweek view of your squad and upcoming fixtures.

Current limitations

Before a Gameweek locks, the public Draft endpoints may not expose the exact editable starting XI. When that happens, the toolkit deliberately shows the owned squad instead of inventing a lineup. The scoring model is also an early version; expected minutes, injury-return timing, role changes and richer start-probability modelling are planned additions.
