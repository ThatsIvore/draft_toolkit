About the toolkit

Decision support for an FPL Draft season

This site turns public FPL and FPL Draft data for this league into season-long decision support: free agents, fixtures, injuries, stash candidates, roster value and potential add/drop moves.

How it works

A scheduled GitHub Actions job collects Draft ownership, public FPL player status and Premier League fixtures. It keeps small ownership and health snapshots, calculates player intelligence, compares free agents with your roster and publishes the processed results here.

Current decision model

v0.5 reduces the bias toward players who simply accumulated a full season of minutes. Historical performance is normalized for playing time, with small samples partially discounted rather than discarded. The model separates Floor—reliable usage and production—from Upside—rate production, attacking involvement and future fixtures.

Sample confidence describes how much historical playing-time evidence supports the comparison. It is not a probability that the recommendation is correct. Before GW1, stronger thresholds apply automatically because new-season roles are less certain.

What the recommendation labels mean

  • CLAIM: a free agent strong enough to add now.
  • STASH: worth using a bench spot for future value before other managers react.
  • WATCH: interesting, but not yet strong enough for a transaction.
  • HOLD: a rostered player worth retaining.
  • REVIEW DROP: actively compare the roster spot with replacements; not an automatic drop.
  • PASS: insufficient current value for a roster spot.

What the waiver labels mean

  • SWAP NOW: a clear same-position improvement with adequate evidence, including no meaningful loss of immediate floor.
  • STASH SWAP: the case is driven mainly by future value and upside.
  • CONSIDER: a positive upgrade exists, but the separation or confidence is not strong enough for a firm recommendation.
  • KEEP ROSTER: the best comparison does not justify changing the roster.

Floor vs Upside: Floor favors players likely to keep producing usable minutes. Upside rewards players whose rate production and attacking involvement indicate more potential than their raw season total may show. Start est. and expected minutes remain toolkit heuristics, not official probabilities.

What the main views are for

  • My Team: your 15 players and roster guidance.
  • Available: free agents sortable by roster upgrade, upside, floor and other signals.
  • League Activity: ownership changes detected between snapshots.
  • Planner: a multi-Gameweek fixture view.

Current limitations

Before a Gameweek locks, public Draft endpoints may not expose the exact editable XI, so the toolkit will not invent one. The model also does not yet ingest press-conference comments, tactical-role changes, set-piece duties or specialist medical reporting.
