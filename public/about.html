About the toolkit

Decision support for an FPL Draft season

This site turns public FPL and FPL Draft data for this league into season-long decision support: free agents, fixtures, injuries, stash candidates, roster value, add/drop comparisons and a recommended starting XI.

How it works

A scheduled GitHub Actions job collects Draft ownership, public FPL player status and Premier League fixtures. It keeps small ownership and health snapshots, calculates player intelligence, compares free agents with your roster, builds a legal Recommended XI and publishes the processed results here.

Current decision model

v0.5.2 preserves a durable 2025/26 preseason performance baseline before the live FPL statistics reset for the new season. Once 2026/27 begins, that prior is blended gradually with new-season minutes and production instead of being replaced after a single match. This prevents established or partial-season players from being judged almost entirely on one Gameweek.

The model separates Floor—reliable usage and production—from Upside—rate production, attacking involvement and future fixtures. Sample confidence describes how much playing-time evidence supports a comparison; it is not a probability that the recommendation is correct.

What the primary waiver labels mean

  • SWAP NOW: a clear same-position improvement with adequate evidence and no meaningful loss of immediate floor.
  • STASH SWAP: reserved for a genuine stash situation: the incoming player has a short-term availability cost, but enough future value and upside to justify acting early.
  • CONSIDER: a positive upgrade exists, but the separation or confidence is not strong enough for a firm recommendation.
  • KEEP ROSTER: the best comparison does not justify changing the roster.

Standalone strength

The secondary player-level label is deliberately non-transactional. Strong means the player grades well in isolation, Future value highlights a stash-type profile, Watch means interesting but not yet compelling, and Depth means the current standalone value is limited. The waiver label above it is the actual roster decision.

Floor vs Upside: Floor favors players likely to keep producing usable minutes. Upside rewards players whose rate production and attacking involvement indicate more potential than their raw season total may show.

Role evidence: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW reflects how much historical playing-time evidence exists. Expected minutes remains a heuristic until enough 2026/27 evidence exists to calibrate a true start model.

Recommended XI · v0.6.1

The Recommended XI is a separate toolkit recommendation and is never presented as your submitted Draft lineup. It searches all legal FPL formations—one goalkeeper, 3–5 defenders, 2–5 midfielders and 1–3 forwards—and selects the highest-scoring legal XI for the next Gameweek.

Start Score is a transparent selection heuristic, not projected FPL points. It combines current availability, the expected-minutes heuristic, the immediate Gameweek fixture difficulty, Floor and Upside. Availability is applied conservatively so a ruled-out player cannot win a starting place purely from historical quality.

Evidence adjustment: v0.6.1 adds a small sample-confidence discount. A LOW-evidence player can still start, but a short historical sample must show a clearer advantage before displacing a better-established option. This avoids treating a small-sample points-per-90 spike as equally trustworthy as a full-season role.

Close calls: when a starter and a same-position bench alternative are within 2.0 Start Score, the site flags the choice as marginal rather than presenting the ranking as decisive. The reserve goalkeeper remains separate and the three outfield substitutes are ordered by Start Score for autosub priority.

What the main views are for

  • My Team: your 15 players, the official-lineup view when public Draft data exposes it, a separate Recommended XI and the full squad list.
  • Available: free agents sortable by best roster upgrade, upside, floor and other signals.
  • League Activity: ownership changes detected between snapshots.
  • Planner: a multi-Gameweek fixture view.

Current limitations

Before a Gameweek locks, public Draft endpoints may not expose the exact editable XI. The toolkit does not invent an official lineup; the Recommended XI is clearly labeled as its own decision-support output. Start Score and expected minutes remain heuristics rather than calibrated projected points. The model also does not yet ingest press-conference comments, tactical-role changes, set-piece duties or specialist medical reporting.
