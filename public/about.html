About the toolkit

Decision support for an FPL Draft season

This site turns public FPL and FPL Draft data for this league into a season-long decision-support view. It is designed to answer practical questions such as who is available, which players have the best upcoming fixtures, who may be worth stashing, and what has changed around the league.

How it works

A scheduled GitHub Actions job collects the latest Draft league ownership, player status and Premier League fixture data. It stores a small ownership snapshot so changes can be detected between runs, calculates player-level intelligence, and publishes the processed results to this GitHub Pages site.

Current scoring model

The v0.2 model remains intentionally transparent rather than a black box. Roster score combines position-relative historical FPL points, the next four Gameweeks of official FPL fixture difficulty, current availability and a usage signal. The usage signal turns public starts and minutes into a rough start-probability and expected-minutes proxy. Stash score keeps more weight on future fixtures and less on immediate availability.

Start probability and expected minutes are estimates produced by this toolkit, not official FPL forecasts. Before meaningful season usage exists, the model falls back conservatively to availability rather than pretending to know a player's role.

What the main views are for

  • My Team: your 15 owned players, upcoming fixtures and player-level intelligence.
  • Available: the free-agent pool, sortable by roster value, stash value, fixtures, points and fitness.
  • League Activity: ownership changes detected between snapshots, intended to become an opponent add/drop radar.
  • Planner: a multi-Gameweek view of your squad and upcoming fixtures.

Current limitations

Before a Gameweek locks, the public Draft endpoints may not expose the exact editable starting XI. When that happens, the toolkit deliberately shows the owned squad instead of inventing a lineup. The usage model does not yet understand tactical role changes, set-piece duties, manager comments or a specific medical return date unless those are reflected in the public FPL status data. Those richer signals are planned future additions.
