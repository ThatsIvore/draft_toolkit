About the toolkit

Decision support for an FPL Draft season

This site turns public FPL and FPL Draft data for this league into season-long decision support: free agents, fixtures, injuries, stash candidates, roster value and potential add/drop moves.

How it works

A scheduled GitHub Actions job collects Draft ownership, public FPL player status and Premier League fixtures. It keeps small ownership and health snapshots, calculates player intelligence, compares free agents with your roster and publishes the processed results here.

Current decision model

v0.5.1 keeps the v0.5 playing-time normalization, Floor and Upside model, but tightens the decision language. On the Available page, the waiver replacement label is now the primary action because it answers the practical question: is this free agent worth replacing someone I actually own? The older CLAIM / WATCH / PASS signal remains visible as a secondary player grade.

Sample confidence describes how much historical playing-time evidence supports a comparison. It is not a probability that the recommendation is correct. Before GW1, stronger thresholds apply automatically because new-season roles are less certain.

What the recommendation labels mean

  • CLAIM: the player grades strongly in isolation.
  • STASH: worth monitoring or carrying for future value despite a current availability cost.
  • WATCH: interesting player-level value, but not yet a strong standalone signal.
  • HOLD: a rostered player worth retaining.
  • REVIEW DROP: actively compare the roster spot with replacements; not an automatic drop.
  • PASS: insufficient standalone player value.

What the waiver labels mean

  • SWAP NOW: a clear same-position improvement with adequate evidence and no meaningful loss of immediate floor.
  • STASH SWAP: reserved for a genuine stash situation: the incoming player has a short-term availability cost, but enough future value and upside to justify using the roster spot early.
  • CONSIDER: a positive upgrade exists, but the separation or confidence is not strong enough for a firm recommendation.
  • KEEP ROSTER: the best comparison does not justify changing the roster.

Floor vs Upside: Floor favors players likely to keep producing usable minutes. Upside rewards players whose rate production and attacking involvement indicate more potential than their raw season total may show.

Role / usage: this is a heuristic strength signal built from public starts/minutes and current availability. It should not be read as a literal probability that a player starts the next match. Expected minutes is also a heuristic until enough current-season evidence exists to calibrate a true start model.

What the main views are for

  • My Team: your 15 players and roster guidance.
  • Available: free agents sortable by best roster upgrade, upside, floor and other signals.
  • League Activity: ownership changes detected between snapshots.
  • Planner: a multi-Gameweek fixture view.

Current limitations

Before a Gameweek locks, public Draft endpoints may not expose the exact editable XI, so the toolkit will not invent one. The model also does not yet ingest press-conference comments, tactical-role changes, set-piece duties or specialist medical reporting.
