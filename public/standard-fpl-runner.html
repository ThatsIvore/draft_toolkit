How do I create the snapshot?
  1. Sign in to the official FPL site and open Pick Team.
  2. Use the toolkit’s two-stage private snapshot helper.
  3. Choose the downloaded standard-fpl-current-team.json here.

Privacy boundary

The snapshot is sent only to this self-hosted runner. It is validated, combined with public FPL data and returned to this tab without being written to disk, browser storage, GitHub or the Draft report.

This first version has no user accounts or remote-access protection. Keep it on your trusted LAN and do not add router port forwarding.
